Root Cause:
  Next.js 15 uses automatic monorepo root discovery heuristics that traverse parent
  directories looking for lockfiles. When traversing upward from `/Users/mushfiq/Desktop/DSA`,
  it discovered the legacy `/Users/mushfiq/package-lock.json` in the user's home folder,
  causing Next.js to infer an ambiguous workspace root and print:
    "⚠ Warning: Next.js inferred your workspace root, but it may not be correct...
     To silence this warning, set outputFileTracingRoot in your Next.js config..."

Safe Resolution Strategy:
  Rather than deleting or mutating parent-directory files outside the project, we explicitly
  configured `outputFileTracingRoot: path.resolve(__dirname)` in `next.config.ts`.
  This tells Next.js definitively that `/Users/mushfiq/Desktop/DSA` is the standalone project root.
