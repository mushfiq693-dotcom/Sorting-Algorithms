     * LEVEL 1 (Simple Algorithms): Bubble, Selection, and Insertion Sort overviews.

   - Quiz Mode (src/components/practice/Quiz.tsx):
     * 4-6 algorithm-accurate MCQs per topic with instant answer feedback and 
       educational explanations.
     * In-session score tracking and celebration confetti.
   - Prediction Exercises (src/components/practice/PredictNext.tsx):
     * Uses real intermediate states from the pure SortOperation[] algorithm runner.
